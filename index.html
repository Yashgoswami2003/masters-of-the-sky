THE AUTHOR

About Aakash Banga

Aakash Banga’s Masters of the Sky: Suryakiran Aerobatic Team explores the people, relationships, and discipline behind one of the Indian Air Force’s most recognisable aerobatic teams. Through the world of the Suryakiran Aerobatic Team (SKAT), the book brings attention to the trust, resilience, precision, and collective commitment required to perform in an environment where every manoeuvre demands exceptional coordination.

For Aakash, military aviation is more than a subject of professional interest. His fascination began during childhood, growing up on Air Force stations, where routine flying and Indian Air Force flypasts left a lasting impression. These early experiences developed into a deeper appreciation for the discipline, skill, and dedication of fighter pilots.
